About usNetwork

Figure out what to do before you start doing it.

You have a question. The work needed to answer it can be designed. Builder and the Library let you do that yourself. When the question needs more, I design and run it with you.

Why work this way

Most hard work gets handled through meetings, opinions and slides, or by jumping straight into execution. The loudest person decides, or nobody does.

A designed session works differently. The question is explicit. The right people are in the room, and the person who decides is one of them. Evidence comes in early. People think on their own before they debate. Alternatives get made tangible. You leave with something usable.

change needs structure

What is a Raw Draft?

A structured cycle for turning uncertainty into something you can test, learn from and decide on.

A workshop might be one Raw Draft.
A Sprint may contain several.
A prototype can be one.
A research cycle can be one.

The principle: make the question tangible enough to expose it to reality.

The cycle of creation

This is why we work in drafts. You make something. Reality pushes back. You structure what survives. Eventually the system can run.

How a session fits

Every workshop or Sprint answers four things: the question, the room, the work and the output. In Builder you pick methods from the Library, sequence them and set the timing. Every part stays editable.
